About EC Structures

Melbourne Trade Work, Done Properly

EC Structures is a Melbourne-based trade business specialising in welding, panel work, patching and caulking. Led by Eoin Callanan and based in Ripponlea, we bring the same care and precision to every job — whether it's a single structural weld or a full precast panel installation.

Our clients trust us because we turn up on time, communicate clearly, keep our sites clean and deliver finishes that stand the test of time. No cut corners, no surprises — just honest, quality workmanship.

  • Service areas: Ripponlea, St Kilda, Elsternwick, Brighton, Melbourne CBD & surrounding suburbs.
